Privacy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page explains what happens to your data when you visit this website. It covers the website only. The use of the Gapsman plugin inside ChatGPT is also subject to OpenAI's own terms and privacy policy.

No cookies, no tracking

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics or advertising tools, and does not load fonts, scripts or videos from third parties. The video, the images and the code are served from this site.

Technical logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every website, the hosting provider receives technical data needed to deliver the pages, such as your IP address, the browser and the time of the request. These logs are kept by the provider for security and operation, for a limited time, and are not used by us to identify you. See the GitHub privacy statement for details.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. This website itself does not collect personal data.
